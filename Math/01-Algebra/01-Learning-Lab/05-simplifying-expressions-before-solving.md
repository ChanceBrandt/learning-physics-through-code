### Updated Understanding

### Updated Combining Like Terms Understanding: 

**Consider:** $4x + 7 + 2x + 3$

We are taking:  
(4 groups of x) + (7 groups of 1) + (2 groups of x) + (3 groups of 1)

Because the terms are being added, we can reorganize the expression so that the groups of x are together and the group of ones are together

(4 groups of x) + (2 groups of x) = 6 groups of x or 6x

(7 groups of ones) + (3 groups of ones) = 10 groups of ones or 10

Combined, you get 6x + 10. 

**Like terms can be combined because they represent groups of the same quantity, including the same exponent.**

## Distributive Property & Factoring  
A rule that lets multiplication apply to every term in a group  
&emsp; **Consider:** 3(4 + 2). This means there are 3 groups of (4 + 2) or (4 + 2) + (4 + 2) + (4 + 2) 
&emsp;&emsp; This can be written as (4 + 2) + (4 + 2) + (4 + 2) or 4 + 2 + 4 + 2 + 4 + 2
&emsp;&emsp; There are 3 groups of 4 and 3 groups of 2, otherwords 3(4) + 3(2) 
&emsp;&emsp; Therefore, 3(4 + 2) = 3(4) + 3(2) 
- Factoring  
    - This is the distrivutive property working backwards. 
    - 4x + 2x = (4 + 2)x  
    - As the expression represented a quantity of x, you can write it as the addition of the coefficients multipled by x

## Subtraction and Negatives 

### Does grouping matter with subtraction?  
**Expression A:** (10 - 3) - 2  
    - 10 - 3 operation happnes first because it is the paranthesis 
    - The value inside the paranthesis is 7 
    - The final result is 7 - 2 which is 5
**Expression B:** 10 - (3 - 2)  
    - 3 - 2 happens first because it is in the paranthesis 
    - The value inside the paranthesis is 1 
    - The final result is 10 - 1 which is 9  
**Changing the group changed the value, therefore it does not have the same associative property as addition** 

### Does order matter with subtraction?  
**Expression A:** 8 - 3  
**Expression B:** 3 - 8  
1. The result of A is 5, the result of B is -5  
2. The answers are NOT the same  
3. Changing the order changed the results  
4. Subtraction does not have the commutative property. 

### Is subtraction really addition?  
**Expression A:** 8 - 3  
**Expression B:** 8 + (-3)  
1. 8 - 3 = 5  
2. 8 + (-3) = 5  
3. The expressions are equivalent  
4. Subtracting a positive number and adding a negative number are equivalent  

**Expression C:** 5 - 9  
**Expression D:** 5 + (-9)  
1. 5 - 9 = -4  
2. 5 + (-9) = -4  
The pattern still works even when the final answer is negative.  

**Once subtraction is rewritten as addition, we can use the commutative and associative properties**

# Definitions:  
**Term:** A term is a piece of an expression usually separated by a mathematical operation.  
&emsp; 3x + 4 + 2x + 5 
&emsp;&emsp; 3x, 4, 2x, and 5 are terms 

**Like Terms:** Like terms are groups that represent the same quantity 
&emsp; 3x + 4 + 2x + 5 
&emsp;&emsp; 3x and 2x are like terms as they represent groups of x
&emsp;&emsp; 4 and 5 are like terms as they represent groups of 1  

**Additive Inverse:** For any number a, its additive inverse is -a.  Therefore a - b = a + (-b) 

**Commutative Property of Addition:** When quantities are added, changing the order of the terms does not change the sum. 
&emsp; 2 + 5 = 5 + 2 = 7  

**Associative Proprty of Addition:** When adding, you can change the grouping of the terms without changing the sum.  
&emsp; (2 + 3) + 4 = 5 + 4 = 9
&emsp; 2 + (3 + 4) = 2 + 7 = 9 

**Factor**: Any quantity being multiplied by another quantity.  
&emsp; 3 x 5 = 15, 3 and 5 are factors

**Coefficient:** The value being multiplied to, or the number of copies of, the variable quantity

**Distributive Property**: A rule that allows you to multiply a factor by each term within a group.
&emsp; a(b + c) = ab + ac
&emsp;&emsp; a is a factor (b + c) is a group, and b/c are the terms within the group. 

**Factoring:** Using the distiributive property backwards to identify a factor shared by multiple terms, place that common factor outside a group, and place the remaining factors inside the group. 
&emsp; ab + ac = a(b + c) 
&emsp;&emsp; ab and ac are the terms, a is the common factor, and (b + c) is what remains after the common factor has been removed from each term.
