## What is a term?  

Considering the same expression $4x + 7 + 2x + 3$

Each individual chunk "4x", "7", "2x", and "3" are all **terms** and generally the terms are separated by mathematical operations. 

**Term:** Individual pieces of an expression generally separated by mathematical operations

## Combining Like Terms

**Consider:** Suppose x = 5 and 4x and 2x  
What does 4x mean if x = 5?  
What does 2x mean?  
Therefore, what does 4x + 2x mean conceptually and arthmetically? 

### My Current Understanding 
- x = 5 means the variable x has the value of 5.
- 4x means that we are taking the value 4 and multiplying it by the variable x. 2x means that we are taking the value 2 and multiplying it by the variable x.
- Since the variable's value is 5, then we would be taking 4 and multipying it by 5 and taking 2 and multipying it by 5. 
- 4x + 2x means that we are taking the term 4x, which is $4 * 5$, and adding it to the term 2x, which is $2 * 5$. 
- This would then be 20 + 10, which equals 30

### Updated Understanding 
- For the expression 4(5) + 2(5), we can think of 4(5) as 4 groups of 5 and 2(5) as 2 groups of 5 
    - This would then be the same as (5 + 5 + 5 + 5) + (5+ 5) 
    - Or 6(5)
- That would mean 4(5) + 2(5) = 6(5) 
- In otherwords, we are taking 4 groups of 5 and adding them to two groups of five, resulting in 6 groups of 5. 

### Updated Combining Like Terms Understanding: 

**Consider:** $4x + 7 + 2x + 3$

We are taking:  
(4 groups of x) + (7 groups of 1) + (2 groups of x) + (3 groups of 1)

Because the terms are being added, we can reorganize the expression so that the groups of x are together and the group of ones are together

(4 groups of x) + (2 groups of x) = 6 groups of x or 6x

(7 groups of ones) + (3 groups of ones) = 10 groups of ones or 10

Combined, you get 6x + 10. 

**Like terms can be combined because they represent groups of the same quantity, including the same exponent.** 

### Coefficients
A coefficient tells us how many copes of the variable quantity we have 

**Example:** 4x means we have 4 copies of x, so 4 is the coefficient.
