# My Goal

Read one Physics Education Paper every one to two weeks 

Answering Questions: 

1. What questions were they asking

2. Why did the researchers think it mattered

3. What did they predict

4. Who did they study 

5. What did they measure

6. How did they analyze it

7. What did they find

8. Does their evidence actually support their conclusion 

9. What limitations do I see

10. What questions does that me **me** what to investigate
